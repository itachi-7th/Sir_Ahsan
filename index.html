Motion

Every rule starts with watching something move. Pull back and release: the path curves because the velocity keeps changing, and the velocity changes because of acceleration.

v = v₀ + at    x = x₀ + v₀t + ½at²

Drag back from the launch point, then release. Gold arrow: velocity. Blue arrow: acceleration (constant, no air resistance). Dots mark equal time intervals. Arrow lengths are scaled for clarity.

Force

A force can’t be seen, only what it does to motion. A steady stream of particles passes an invisible source. Move it and watch the paths change.

a = F / m
Touch or move inside the frame to place the source. An illustrative field, not a precise simulation.

Waves

Where two waves meet, they add. Crest on crest grows taller; crest on trough cancels. Drag the sources and change the wavelength to see the pattern rearrange.

y = A sin(kr − ωt)    y = y₁ + y₂
Blue: crests. Gold: troughs. The wave speed stays fixed; k = 2π/λ.

Gravity

Mass changes how everything nearby moves. These bodies follow Newton’s law of gravitation around the central mass. Drag the mass and the orbits answer.

a = GM / r²
The bent grid is the familiar rubber-sheet analogy, an artistic picture. It is not a General Relativity calculation.
